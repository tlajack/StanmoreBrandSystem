The marks

Three primary lockups, plus three one-colour versions for restricted processes. The 2026 mark is the standard going forward; the previous unboxed wordmark stays in circulation on existing stock and equipment until it is replaced, but never appears alongside the 2026 mark in a single piece.

Primary

The badge lockup — hex monogram over the wordmark. The standard mark in this concept, used wherever the stacked proportion fits: covers, vehicle sides, booth graphics.

Boxed horizontal — light ground

The 2026 mark, and the default for print collateral, signage and booth graphics. Preferred on white and light grounds; on black it holds as long as the red and the wordmark keep their contrast.

Decal — stacked

Two-panel lockup with the phone number. Vehicle doors, equipment flanks and anywhere the number has to read at distance.

Tagline lockup

Optional, and never part of the logo artwork. Set it as live type only.

Barlow Condensed Bold, all caps, tracking 140, set at roughly one third of the wordmark's cap height. Centred on the mark, one clear-space unit below it.

The skewed red bar is optional and sits at the tagline's cap height. Use the tagline on covers, booth panels and vehicle rears — not on business cards or spec sheets, where it competes with contact information.

One-colour

For single-ink print, cut vinyl, embroidery, engraving and any process that cannot hold two colours. Never substitute a one-colour version where the full mark will reproduce.
Black

White

02

Clear space & minimum size

Clear space on all four sides equals one quarter of the mark's box height. Nothing enters that zone — no type, no rule, no photo edge, no fold or trim.

Business card, letterhead32 mm / 1.25 in wide min.
Flyer, brochure, quote form50 mm / 2 in wide min.
Equipment decal300 mm / 12 in wide min.
Vehicle door450 mm / 18 in wide min.
Booth banner, towerOne third of panel width

Below the minimum, drop the black keyline on the boxed mark — at small sizes it fills in and muddies the letterforms.

03

Misuse

✕Never re-set the wordmark in another typeface. It is drawn artwork, not live type.
✕Never stretch, condense or re-slant. Scale proportionally only.
✕Never recolour. Red, black and white only — no gradients, no metallics, no brand-partner colours.
✕Never add drop shadows, bevels, outlines or outer glows.
✕Never place the boxed mark on a red field, or the unboxed white mark on light grey.
✕Never lock partner logos inside the Stanmore box. Run them as a separate row on a white strip, clear of the mark.
✕Never rotate. The only exception is a deliberate vertical spine on a narrow format.
✕Never rebuild the mark from a screenshot or a JPG. Always start from the supplied vector.
✕
All-red mark

Not recommended. Red on white gives the wordmark too little contrast to read at distance or at small sizes.

Where only one ink is available, use the black or white one-colour mark instead.

06

Graphic elements

The red rule

A solid red bar anchoring the bottom or top edge of a panel. Weight is 3% of the panel height. One per piece.

The angled cut

One 14° cut across the format, black field to open ground, with a red edge on the cut. Divides a cover, a card front or a banner without adding a second element.

The 14° slant

The wordmark's slant, reused as a repeating bar motif. Always 14° from vertical, always leaning right. Accent only — never behind body copy.

The halftone texture

Halftone dots following the 14° slant, multiplied over a Stanmore Red field. The texture is supplied as black artwork and laid over the red, so the red underneath stays PMS 485 C. Never apply it over any colour but red.

Use it to give a large red field some weight: booth panels, flyer covers, the back of a card. Keep type off the densest areas, or run type on a clean red or white panel sitting on top. Scale it so the dots stay visible at viewing distance rather than tiling small.

Use one element per piece. The identity does its work through flat colour blocking and hard edges, so stacking a rule, a cut and a slant on one layout reads as noise rather than as Stanmore.

Texture in use

The texture is a ground, not an element — a rule, a cut or the mark can sit on top of it.

Vehicle panel. The badge and wordmark run inline on a clean red field spanning the cargo side, with the hood wrap picking up the red. The field runs untextured — the hex badge carries the visual weight the halftone used to provide.

Business card

Front is a textured red field carrying the mark, with the partner row on a white strip along the foot. Back keeps contact type on white and takes the texture only in the red band at the foot, so nothing reads over the dots.

Partner marks run as a single row along the bottom edge, equal height, in their own colours on a white strip — never dropped straight onto the texture.

07

Applications

Business card
3.5 × 2 in · 16 pt matte · 1/8 in bleed

Front carries the mark alone. Back carries name, title, email, branch address and both phone numbers, set left, with the QR at upper right. Name in red, surname in black.

Equipment & vehicle decal
Cast vinyl · 5 yr outdoor · contour cut

Stacked lockup, red panel over white panel, phone number always present. Keep the decal off panel seams, hinges and rivets. Match the same red on every unit in the fleet.

Flyer & spec sheet
8.5 × 11 in · 0.5 in margins

Mark top left, red rule at the foot with phone and web. Equipment photography sits full-bleed or on white — never on a red field, which fights the machines.

Trade show
Read at 20 ft · one message per panel

Black ground, white type, red accent. Mark in the top third of each panel above sightline. Partner brands run as a single row along the base, equal height, on white.

Vendor checklist

01Vector artwork only: EPS or PDF for print, SVG for screen. No PNG or JPG logos.
02All type converted to outlines before release.
03CMYK for process print, spot for two-colour jobs, RGB never sent to press.
041/8 in bleed and a 1/8 in safety margin on every trimmed piece.
05Raster images 300 dpi at final size; large-format banners 100 dpi at final size.
06Hard-copy or drawdown proof on the first run of any new red application.
07Tagline set as live type, never pulled from a logo file: LIFTING YOU SINCE '62.
08Barlow Condensed and Barlow supplied with the artwork package; do not substitute.

Questions on any application, or a format not covered here, go to the marketing contact before the job goes to press.
